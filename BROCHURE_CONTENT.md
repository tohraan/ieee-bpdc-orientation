## Typography Notes for Reproducing the Brochure

- **All section headings** (WHY JOIN IEEE?, WHO CAN JOIN?, OUR VISION, OUR MISSION, MEMBERSHIP BENEFITS, WHAT WE HAVE ORGANIZED) use **Bebas Neue** — they are naturally uppercase.
- **"About Us" badge** uses Inter 700, uppercase, inside a filled navy pill with white text.
- **Stat numbers** (1884, 460000+, etc.) use Bebas Neue or JetBrains Mono.
- **Stat labels** (FOUNDED, MEMBERS, etc.) use Inter 600, uppercase, `letter-spacing: 0.08em`.
- **Bullet points** in the membership benefits and "Why Join" sections use `color: var(--color-cyan-400)` for the bullet symbol.
- **Highlighted words** in the About Us body (1884, 460000, 190+ countries, 140+ years) use `color: var(--color-cyan-400)` and `font-weight: 700`.
- **Discipline names** in "Who Can Join?" (CS, ECE, ECOM, etc.) use `color: var(--color-cyan-400)`.
