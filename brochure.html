About Us

Founded in 1884, IEEE (Institute of Electrical & Electronics Engineers) is the world's largest technical professional organisation, with over 460000 members across 190+ countries.

With a rich legacy of over 140+ years, IEEE has been advancing technology for humanity by fostering innovation, research, professional development & global collaboration.

IEEE aims to inspire innovation, foster technological excellence, and create solutions that positively impact society through engineering and technology.

Why Join IEEE?

IEEE BPDC is one of the most active student chapters on campus, providing students with opportunities to learn beyond the classroom through practical experiences, interdisciplinary collaborations, and industry engagement.

As an IEEE member, you can gain access to:

  • Global professional network
  • Workshops & hands-on learning
  • Industry expert talks
  • Technical competitions & hackathons
  • International conferences & research papers

Who can Join?

Open to students from all disciplines !!

IEEE welcomes students from CS, ECE, ECOM, CHEMICAL, BIOTECH, MECHANICAL, MATHEMATICS and more.

No prior experience required- just curiosity & willingness to learn !
